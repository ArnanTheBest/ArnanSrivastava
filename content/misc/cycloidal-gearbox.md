A cycloidal gearbox, sectioned to show the reduction stage inside: an eccentric input drives a cycloidal disc against a ring of pins, producing a large reduction in a compact, coaxial package. Cycloidal drives are attractive where you want high reduction and low backlash without the radial size of a comparable planetary stage.
