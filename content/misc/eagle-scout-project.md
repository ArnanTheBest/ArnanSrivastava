StoryPoint is an assisted living facility. My connection there is through Robotics - I was the team's former outreach lead, and every Sunday we helped residents with technical issues. It's a strange thing to see how challenging some of that is for the residents and how simple it is for us. StoryPoint had a huge storage area that was an absolute mess: cheap plastic shelves, worn down, badly cluttered. I decided to redo their shelving completely. I got a layout of the room and designed a shelf system in CAD: [Shelf CAD, Onshape](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/df4fd518527e9c01120f8867/w/4e6e17d429e7294e527ed4c0/e/e1cebddf139875ace72b4f8a).
