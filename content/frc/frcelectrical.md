FRCElectrical.org is a resource site I founded for FRC teams on the electrical side of robot build - control system layout, making reliable connections, mechanism wiring, and the tools and crimps that actually work, plus an educator's handbook for teams teaching this to new members. I wrote most of the site myself, even though I'm not a programmer - two friends of mine, who are honestly some of the best people I know to work with, co-founded it with me and handled the actual programming, patiently teaching me how the code worked along the way so I could keep contributing. We've since built our own server and grown to a number of contributors beyond the three of us. Everything on it was written with zero AI involved.
