A generic block representation for electronics components, made for the same reason as the MK4n block above - block CAD was just starting to become popular at the time, and this was meant to reduce time spent doing full-detail CAD for electronics placement on a robot. It was added to FRCDesignLib a day after it was created. Getting it to work fully took a lot of messing with Onshape configurations. [Block electronics CAD, Onshape](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/1af0147813f00d76d42d91c4/w/047c0a8a5190a30458b86b16/e/300b557dbb32844abcd9f932).
