A parametric gift case, printed in silk PLA for its reflective, metallic-looking finish without actually using any metal. The same base design has been reconfigured for different gifts - a mirror compact, a coin and gold bar display case, and an engraved anniversary box - just by changing the internal cavity and the lid text.
