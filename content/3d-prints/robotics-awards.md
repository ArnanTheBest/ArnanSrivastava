These awards were made for the rookies on my team, and they hold a special place in my heart. I love creating, and making physical objects and contraptions for my own learning is swell.

But creation comes in other forms too: creating a better team for the rookies, creating better standards, and melding the minds of the rookies so that they can take my place and swim without having to sink as I once did.

Making these was a small gesture to show how proud I was of every single one of them for getting good at whatever they set their minds to.
