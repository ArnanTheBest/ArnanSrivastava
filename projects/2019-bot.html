What is block CAD?

Block CAD is modeling a mechanism as simple solids - blocks, cylinders, basic shapes standing in for real parts - before any actual part geometry exists. It lets you check layout, clearances, and motion quickly, without spending hours modeling bearings and fasteners for a design that might still change. The tradeoff is discipline: it only works if you actually keep it low-fidelity until the layout is locked in, which is exactly what I didn't do the first time.
