2017 bot

Built after cataloging everything that went wrong with the 2016 practice bot - which was, honestly, everything. This one was a fine robot: a hopper/shooter and a gear intake on a real drivetrain. The mistakes were fewer but still real - there were too many belts, and the compliant wheels were the wrong call, since they traditionally explode at high speed. Better than 2016 by a wide margin, but still a rough robot overall.
