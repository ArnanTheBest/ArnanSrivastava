Wire holder

A springy, universal wire holder, inspired by 5667M's Jason Simon's 2025 end effector. The whole project was made for my mom, who needed help keeping her wires managed and organized.

Non-universal, non-springy version

non-universal, non-springy wire holder

A simpler, rigid version without the spring-loaded universal fit of the main design. This one is meant to be printed in TPU rather than a rigid filament, trading the mechanical spring for material flexibility instead.
